# Rice Tungro Disease

**Causal Agent / Pathogen**: *Rice tungro bacilliform virus (RTBV) & Rice tungro spherical virus (RTSV) — Vector: Green Leafhopper (Nephotettix virescens)*  
**Category**: Viral Disease (Insect Vector-borne)  
**Diagnostic Source**: ICAR - National Rice Research Institute (Pocket Diary No. 4)  
**Guide Reference**: Pre-disposing Factors: Page 16 (Book Page 11) | Management: Page 20 (Book Page 15)


---

## Pre-disposing Factors

* Early rain during April–May.
* Late planting (after 15th August).
* Nursery raising near ratoon crops or infected stubble.
* Appearance and population build-up of the vector **Green Leafhopper (GLH)** within 50 days of transplanting.

---

## Specific Management Practices

**Agronomic Practice**:
  * Avoid late planting (beyond the 2nd week of August), especially in Eastern Indian states.
  * Destroy ratoon crops and volunteer rice plants near nurseries to remove virus reservoir.
**Vector (Green Leafhopper) Control**:
  * Give need-based foliar spray targeting GLH vectors using:
    * **Imidacloprid 17.8SL @ 0.25ml/litre of water**, OR
    * **Thiamethoxam 25WG @ 0.2g/litre of water**.

---

### Common Management Practices (Cultural & Preventive)
* **Healthy Seeds**: Use healthy seeds selected from a disease-free crop.
* **Balanced Fertilizer Application**: Apply recommended doses of fertilizers in split doses. **Avoid heavy doses of Nitrogen**.
* **Field Sanitation**: Maintain field sanitation by destroying infected plant parts and crop residues.
* **Weed Host Eradication**: Remove and destroy weed hosts along field bunds and irrigation channels.
* **Deep Summer Ploughing**: Adopt deep summer ploughing followed by summer flooding of the field to expose and kill dormant soil-borne propagules.
* **Organic Amendment**: Apply cow dung-based Farm Yard Manure (FYM) to enrich antagonistic soil microflora.
* **Spacing**: Avoid close spacing / dense planting to maintain aeration within the canopy.
* **Irrigation Water Management**: Avoid the flow of irrigation water from an infected field into a healthy field.
* **Need-Based Chemicals**: Use chemical control measures strictly based on economic threshold levels and field scouting.
* **Chemical Seed Treatment**: Treat seeds with **Carbendazim 50WP @ 2g/kg seed** to prevent seed-borne infections.
* **Biocontrol Seed Treatment**: Treat seeds with biocontrol agents:
  * *Trichoderma viride* 1WP @ 4g/kg seed, OR
  * *Pseudomonas fluorescens* 0.5WP @ 10g/kg seed.


---

### Application & Safety Guidelines (Page 20 / Book Page 15)
* **Water Volume for Spraying**:
  * **Hand-operated knapsack sprayer**: Use **200 litres** of water per acre of land.
  * **Power / Automatic sprayer**: Use **80 litres** of water per acre of land.
* **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Protect hands, face, and eyes while mixing and spraying fungicides/pesticides.
* **Post-Application Hygiene**: Wash hands, face, and eyes thoroughly with clean water and soap immediately after spraying.
* **Storage & Safety**: Always store chemicals in their original containers out of reach of children and farm livestock.
