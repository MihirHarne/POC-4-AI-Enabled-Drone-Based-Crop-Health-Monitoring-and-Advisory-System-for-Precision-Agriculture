# Rice Disease Diagnostic & Management Guide

This directory contains standalone clinical advisory profiles for 11 major rice diseases documented in the **ICAR-National Rice Research Institute (NRRI, Cuttack)** *Diagnostic Guide for Rice Diseases* (Pocket Diary No. 4).

Each markdown file links the observed field symptoms, pre-disposing environmental conditions (pages 6–16), and specific chemical/cultural management practices (pages 17–20).

## Universal Cultural & Spraying Guidelines

### Common Practices (Page 17)
1. **Healthy Seed Selection**: Always obtain certified, disease-free seed lots.
2. **Balanced Nutrition**: Apply fertilizer strictly according to soil testing. Split nitrogen doses; avoid excess urea.
3. **Field Sanitation & Summer Ploughing**: Destroy weed hosts and stubble; practice deep summer ploughing followed by solarization/flooding.
4. **Water Flow Management**: Do not channel irrigation water from infected rice plots into healthy fields.
5. **Seed Treatment**: 
   * Chemical: Carbendazim 50WP @ 2g/kg seed.
   * Bio-agent: *Trichoderma viride* 1WP @ 4g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* 0.5WP @ 10g/kg seed.

### Spray Calibration & Safety (Page 20)
* **Water Dilution**: 200 L water/acre for manual knapsack sprayers; 80 L water/acre for power/tractor-mounted sprayers.
* **Safety & PPE**: Wear gloves, masks, and eye protection. Wash thoroughly after spraying and keep agrochemicals out of reach of children.
